Any position that is not cached is built live from the SPHEREx archive (IRSA). The archive search usually takes from under a minute to a few minutes, depending on the archive; then the first 8 images appear, chosen to spread over the observation dates and the detector bands, and you can load more (up to 102) when you want them. Each image is about 4.4 MB to download (the sizes and waits shown are rough figures from a few runs, not guarantees). Frames appear as they arrive and the brightness scale is refined as more come in. The cached targets are stored at 0.2° only: any other field of view is built live, also for a cached target, and wider fields take longer. At 0.5° and 1° part of an image can be empty where the field runs off the edge of the detector.

The requested position is the centre of the image; tick "Mark centre" to circle it. North is up, east is left; frames are not rotated or flipped. A moving target can be off-centre and shifts between frames: look for something that hops across the field while the stars stay put. Images are shown pixel-for-pixel with nearest-neighbour scaling; zoom magnifies the centre of the same pixels and does not resample or restretch them.
