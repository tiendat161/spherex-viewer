Position (ICRS, degrees)

Any other position is built live from the SPHEREx archive (IRSA): up to 40 frames spread evenly over the observation dates. The archive is slow, so expect a few minutes; frames appear as they arrive and the brightness scale is refined as more come in. The cached targets are stored at 0.2° only: any other field of view is built live, also for a cached target, and wider fields take longer. At 0.5° and 1° part of an image can be empty where the field runs off the edge of the detector.

Wide field: the wavelength shown is exact only at the centre of the image. SPHEREx observes each position on the detector at its own wavelength, so towards the edges of a field this wide the light is from a different wavelength than the one shown. How large the difference is has not been measured for this tool.

The requested position is the centre of the image; tick "Mark centre" to circle it. North is up, east is left; frames are not rotated or flipped. A moving target can be off-centre and shifts between frames: look for something that hops across the field while the stars stay put. Images are shown pixel-for-pixel with nearest-neighbour scaling; zoom magnifies the centre of the same pixels and does not resample or restretch them.
